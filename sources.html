Conversions and provenance

Mass-specific heat capacity = reported molar heat capacity / molar mass. Water uses the NIST liquid Shomate equation evaluated at 298.15 K. Values are rounded to four decimal places for entry. For boiling ranges, the lower endpoint is used. Density and phase values: Aldrich Physical Properties of Solvents. Ethanol density uses Sigma-Aldrich E7023 (0.789 g/mL at 20 °C) instead of the inconsistent ethanol density in the general table. Data checked 16 November 2026.

Kinetic basis

IUPAC: Arrhenius equation · IUPAC: activation energy. These define the model; no universal activation energy is assumed.

User inputs

Rate enhancement, stimulus power, efficiencies, heat-loss coefficient, vessel capacity and operating durations are user inputs. Initial example values are illustrative. No experimental performance claim is attached to them.
